## Data we process

Depending on how you use SongPilot, we may process:

- **Account data:** email address, account identifier and any sign-in-provider information needed to authenticate you.
- **Profile data:** username, skill level, instruments, genres, favourite artists, collaboration preferences and Pro status.
- **Song and project data:** song titles, arrangements, chords, tablature, musical settings and visibility choices.
- **Collaboration data:** collaboration requests, messages, participants and related project references.
- **Microphone data:** SongPilot may access the microphone only after your permission to support chord/pitch capture. Audio is processed for that feature; confirm and document whether any raw audio is transmitted or retained before release.
- **Technical data:** device, diagnostic and security information made available by the app platform or service providers.

## Why we process data

We use data to provide authentication, save and synchronize projects, enable discovery and collaboration when you opt in, operate audio features, maintain security, provide support and comply with legal obligations.

## Sharing and service providers

SongPilot uses Supabase to provide authentication and database services. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the service, when you direct us to share content through collaboration or public visibility, or when required by law. Do not add advertising, analytics, payment, AI, crash-reporting or storage providers without updating this policy and store disclosures.

## Visibility and collaboration

Projects marked `public` or `collab_open`, and profiles with "Open to collaboration" enabled, may be visible to other SongPilot users. Keep a project private if you do not want it discoverable.

## Retention and deletion

We retain data while your account is active and as needed to operate SongPilot, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. You can request account deletion from **Profile > Request account deletion** in the app. The request is processed through a protected server-side procedure that removes the account and associated data according to the applicable retention requirements.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy of your data, and complain to a supervisory authority. Contact us using the email above to exercise applicable rights.
